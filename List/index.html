  1. Apple

    Apple

    An apple is a round, edible fruit produced by the apple tree (Malus domestica).
    It belongs to the rose family (Rosaceae). Apples come in different colors, including red, green, and yellow.
    They have a sweet or slightly sour taste and are rich in fiber, vitamins, and antioxidants.
    Apples are widely grown and eaten around the world.




  2. Banana

    Banana

    A banana is a long, curved fruit that grows in clusters on banana plants.
    It has a yellow peel when ripe and soft, sweet flesh inside. Bananas are rich in potassium, vitamin B6, vitamin C, and fiber.
    They provide energy and are enjoyed by people around the world.
    Bananas can be eaten fresh or used in smoothies, cakes, and other delicious foods.

  3. Mango

    Mango

    Mango is a sweet and juicy tropical fruit. It has green, yellow, or red skin and soft, yellow-orange flesh inside.
    Mangoes have a delicious taste and a pleasant smell. They are rich in vitamins A and C, which help keep our bodies healthy.
    Mangoes can be eaten fresh or used to make juice, smoothies, and desserts.
